# ShiftLedger

A simple and focused **Shift & Comp-Off Tracker** designed to help you manage work shifts, track holiday and weekend work, and maintain a clear record of earned and used compensatory offs.

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Monthly Shift Calendar** — View and manage your shifts in an easy-to-use calendar.
- 🕒 **Custom Shift Timings** — Configure default timings for different shift types.
- 🎉 **Holiday Tracking** — Mark holidays and record shifts worked on those days.
- 📊 **Comp-Off Ledger** — Automatically track comp-offs earned from eligible weekend or holiday shifts.
- ✅ **Usage Tracking** — Mark earned comp-offs as used and monitor your remaining balance.
- 🔄 **Month Navigation** — Access and review shift history across different months.
- ☁️ **Cloud Sync** — Keep your shift and comp-off data synced across devices.
- 🔐 **Authentication** — Sign in securely to access your personal workspace.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML**
- **CSS**
- **JavaScript**
- **Supabase**
- **Font Awesome** for icons

## 📌 What ShiftLedger Does

ShiftLedger brings shift tracking and comp-off management into one simple workspace. Record your work schedule, track eligible weekend and holiday shifts, and maintain a clear overview of your **earned, used, and remaining comp-offs**.
